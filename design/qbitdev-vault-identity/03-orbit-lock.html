The orbit closes over the Q: a lock that opens for one command you approved, and for nobody in the chat.

The mark

One of the qbitdev orbits becomes the shackle of a padlock and the Q becomes its body. It reads as "lock" at a glance, which helps outside the developer crowd; the cost is a taller, less square mark.

Color

Measured from the qbitdev logo: near-black space, a blue and a green that glow, frost white for text. The blue-to-green gradient belongs to the mark and to selection only; flat colors carry everything else.
