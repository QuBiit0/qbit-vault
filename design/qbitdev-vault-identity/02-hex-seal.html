The mark

The hexagon badge from the qbitdev hoodie becomes the seal of the vault, with the brand Q inside. Its three vertices carry the orbit electrons. A hexagon holds its shape at 16 px and in box-drawing characters, so the terminal and the favicon use the same mark.

Color

Measured from the qbitdev logo: near-black space, a blue and a green that glow, frost white for text. The blue-to-green gradient belongs to the mark and to selection only; flat colors carry everything else.
