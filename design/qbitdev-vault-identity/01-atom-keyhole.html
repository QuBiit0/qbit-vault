The mark

The qbitdev Q, unchanged in shape and gradient, with its counter turned into a keyhole: same family, one new idea. The orbits stay from the master brand; on small sizes they drop and the Q stands alone.

Color

Measured from the qbitdev logo: near-black space, a blue and a green that glow, frost white for text. The blue-to-green gradient belongs to the mark and to selection only; flat colors carry everything else.
