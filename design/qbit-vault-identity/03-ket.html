QBit Vault

A secret held in a closed state: your agent can use it, and the act of looking is exactly what it cannot do.

The mark

The ket |q⟩ from quantum notation, for the QBit name, sealed inside a sphere. The ion dot on the orbit is the only part that moves: a secret in use, never one on display.

Color

A deep night violet for the closed state, lilac paper, one bright violet for actions and an ion blue for a secret in use; ember only for errors.
