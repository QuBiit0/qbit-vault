The mark

A Q drawn as a lens with a redaction bar across it: what passes through QBit Vault comes out with the secret covered. The bar is cobalt in color, ink when printed in one color.

Color

Cool paper and deep ink, so the bar reads as ink on a document. One saturated color, cobalt, carries every action and every redaction; green and amber only report outcomes.
